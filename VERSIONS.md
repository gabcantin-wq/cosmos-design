## Publication rule (2026-09-26)

Only numbered, ordered, LLM-ready corpus files are versioned, without the author's verbatims.
The author's words are preserved locally, available under his supervision only.
Kept local: verbatims, working sessions, `sources/`, manifests, changelogs,
internal notes and archives.
