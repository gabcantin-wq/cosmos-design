Prism for data center operations

See the signal. Understand the situation. Act with care.

Bring selected video streams and facility sensors into a shared operational picture. Prism can relate what is happening to the surrounding context, prepare a response and carry the lesson into the next event.

A practical first stepBegin with a small set of permitted feeds, sensor points and agreed responses. Verify that slice in the client's environment, then extend it as needs and evidence grow.

What matters for this need

Relevant capabilities.

01

Connect the right signals

Select the video streams and sensor readings that matter to one operating situation, rather than importing every system at once.

02

Read events in context

Compare timing, source and nearby measurements so an alert has an explanation and an uncertainty level.

03

Keep action bounded

Notify, suggest or carry out only the responses agreed for that site. Operators keep the decisions that need human judgment.

A situation in practice

Start with one operating area.

A first pilot can be useful without covering an entire facility. Its behavior is defined with the operations team and checked against the systems already on site.

  1. SelectChoose a limited set of camera views, sensor points and events to examine.
  2. UnderstandRelate a detected event to current measurements, recent changes and the available visual context.
  3. Respond and refineTest an approved notification or response, review operator feedback, then expand the scope deliberately.
